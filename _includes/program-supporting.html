Supporting communities

GiD is proud to be actively supporting open source communities through contribution, sponsoring foundations and special projects, awarding support through the FOSS Fund, and more.

FOSS Fund

The GiD Open Source Software (FOSS) Fund provides a direct way for GiD contributors to participate in the nomination and selection process to help communities and projects they are passionate about.

More information about the fund, including recent projects that have received funds, can be found at https://aka.ms/GiDfossfund.
